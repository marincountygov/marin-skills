# Mediums

Review workflow for how County identity applies to a specific channel. Requirements are canonical in `marin-digital-standards/brand/mediums.md`.

## Review checklist by medium

- **Email signature** — concise, follows County identity guidance, no unnecessary quotes/badges/decorative images/oversized graphics.
- **Presentation** — title/section/end slides use consistent County identity, slides aren't overcrowded, charts/diagrams are clearly labeled and County-styled, background/text pairings follow `color.md`.
- **Social media** — avatar/profile identity uses the approved treatment, post graphic is legible on mobile, logo isn't overused when the platform avatar already establishes identity.
- **Video** — opening card, lower thirds, and end card follow one consistent system; logo isn't distorted by animation or motion; title/name treatments are consistent across a series.
- **Reports/flyers/one-pagers** — title, date, department/program owner, and contact/next-step information are clear; identity is present but not oversized; tables/callouts/sidebars improve scanning rather than cluttering the page.

## General checks that apply everywhere

- Is official identity used correctly for this medium?
- Is the medium-specific template followed, when one exists?
- Is the layout aligned and uncluttered?
- Are color and typography consistent with County guidance?
- Are photos, icons, and graphics appropriate for a public County communication?
- Are approvals or official source assets needed before this can publish?

## Output

Fold into `SKILL.md`'s standard review output format under "Layout/media issues," with a medium-specific note.
