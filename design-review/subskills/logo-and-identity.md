# Logo and identity

Review workflow for County of Marin logo treatment, lockups, placement, scale, co-branding, department marks, social media avatars, and video identity. Requirements are canonical in `marin-digital-standards/brand/logo.md` and `identity.md`.

## Review questions

- Is the official logo file being used?
- Is the chosen logo version (Primary, Secondary, logotype, symbol) appropriate for the space?
- Is the logo legible at final size?
- Is there adequate clear space?
- Has the logo been altered in any way — stretched, distorted, rotated, recolored, cropped, outlined, shadowed?
- Does the footer or end card include required County identity where applicable?
- For co-branding: does department expression preserve County family identity, or is official department guidance needed to confirm?

## Common findings

- Logo recreated or approximated instead of using official artwork.
- Clear space violated by adjacent text, graphics, or another logo.
- Wrong logo variant for the background (e.g., black-line version on a dark background).
- Below minimum size for the variant in use.
- Department website missing the required County Primary Logo footer link.
