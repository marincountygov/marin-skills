# Video and audio

Public videos, recorded meetings, training videos, livestreams, podcasts, webinars, public service announcements. The player/interaction requirements are covered by `marin-digital-standards/accessibility/keyboard.md` and `focus.md`; this subskill covers caption, transcript, and audio-description review specifically.

## Checks

- **Captions** for all spoken dialogue and meaningful sound, accurate and synchronized, with speakers identified when more than one is present, readable against the background, and not exceeding a reasonable line length.
- **Transcripts** for audio-only content, and for video content when useful — including speaker identification and meaningful sound/visual context, available near the media or clearly linked, in an accessible format.
- **Audio description** (or an equivalent text alternative) whenever meaningful information is presented only visually — on-screen instructions, charts, maps, demonstrations, names/titles/locations shown as text only, or a critical action with no narration.
- **Player controls** that are keyboard-operable and have accessible names.
- **Text overlays** that meet contrast requirements and stay on screen long enough to read.
- No flashing content that could trigger seizures.

## Review workflow

1. Identify whether the media is audio-only, video-only, or audio-video — this determines which of the above apply.
2. Check for captions, a transcript, and any audio-description need.
3. Review on-screen text, contrast, flashing content, and timing.
4. Confirm the player itself is accessible.
5. Provide remediation steps before publication — captioning and audio description are usually not feasible to add same-day, so flag early.

## Common findings

- Video published with auto-generated captions never reviewed for accuracy.
- Audio-only content (a podcast episode) with no transcript at all.
- Critical on-screen instructions with no narration and no audio description.
- Player controls unreachable by keyboard.

## Output format

Use the standard finding format from `SKILL.md`. Always include a clear publication-readiness recommendation — captioning/transcription lead time is usually the limiting factor.
